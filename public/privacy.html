JASLIN Privacy Policy

JASLIN Mining Game • Built on TON

Effective date: 31 August 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how JASLIN (“JASLIN”, “we”, “our”, or “the Game”) handles information when you use the JASLIN Telegram Bot and Mini App. By using JASLIN, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We May Process

To operate the Game, we may process:

  • Telegram information, such as Telegram user ID, username, first name, and other basic profile information made available to the Mini App.
  • Game information, such as JASLIN balance, mining sessions, Core level, rewards, referral activity, task progress, and related in-game records.
  • TON wallet information, such as the public wallet address you connect or save in the Game.
  • Transaction information, including withdrawal requests, transaction status, amounts, and public blockchain transaction data.
  • Technical and security information needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and prevent abuse of the service.
JASLIN does not ask for or store your wallet seed phrase, recovery phrase, or private key. Never share those credentials with anyone.

3. How We Use Information

  • To create and maintain your JASLIN game account.
  • To calculate mining rewards, levels, holding requirements, referrals, and other game functions.
  • To connect game activity with a TON wallet when you choose to provide one.
  • To process and record eligible withdrawals when withdrawals are enabled.
  • To detect fraud, abuse, automated misuse, duplicate requests, and security threats.
  • To maintain, debug, improve, and protect the Game.

4. Service Providers and Infrastructure

JASLIN relies on third-party infrastructure and services to operate, including Telegram, hosting providers, database providers, TON blockchain infrastructure, and blockchain/RPC or market-data services. These providers may process limited technical information according to their own privacy policies.

5. Blockchain Data

TON is a public blockchain. Public wallet addresses and on-chain transactions may be permanently visible to anyone and may not be deleted or changed by JASLIN. Do not use a wallet address if you do not want its public blockchain activity to be associated with your use of the Game.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared only when necessary to operate the service, comply with applicable legal obligations, investigate abuse or security incidents, or protect users and the Game.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Game, maintain security and transaction records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and comply with applicable obligations. Some blockchain records cannot be deleted because they are stored on a public decentralized network.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect JASLIN systems and account data. However, no online service or blockchain system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Choices

You may stop using the JASLIN Bot or Mini App at any time. You may also disconnect your TON wallet from your wallet application. Public blockchain records created before disconnection will remain on-chain.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as JASLIN develops. The effective date shown at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are published.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions about JASLIN, contact us through the official Telegram bot: @JaslinEarnBot.
